# Sharing and license

Polysite is **source-available**, not open source. The license covers the whole product,
including the starter theme and anything you build from it. Section 6 of the license is
about themes and plugins; this page is a plain-language summary. The license text governs.

The licence is under review before the first public release, so the terms below, in particular the
rule about selling themes, may change. Check `LICENSE.md` in the version you are using.

## What you may do

- **Build themes** for your own sites, or for your own organization's sites, without
  asking anyone.
- **Share a theme free of charge**, for non-commercial purposes: publish it in a
  repository, hand it to a colleague, or submit it to a community directory if one is
  offered, on the terms published for submissions.
- **Keep the copyright** in the original work you add. The license doesn't give anyone
  ownership of your theme.

## What you may not do, for now

Unless you have a written agreement, you may not **sell** a theme, charge for downloading or
accessing it, put it in a paid subscription, or bundle it into something you sell. Selling
themes may be allowed in the future through a program or a separate agreement.

## When you share a theme

- Include the `LICENSE.md` file. `new-theme.sh` copies it for you. A theme must carry a copy
  of the license, and it stays under the license for the Polysite code it contains.
- Say in the README that the theme was made from the Polysite starter.
- Don't use the Polysite name or logo as if your theme were an official one. The name and
  logo are not licensed for your own use.
- Use only content you have the right to use. Demo content should be fictional, never a real
  organization's branding.

## If you're not sure

Ask before relying on a use you're unsure about. For anything the license doesn't allow, such
as selling themes, contact the licensor through the
[`polysites`](https://github.com/polysites) organization on GitHub.
