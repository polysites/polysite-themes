# polysite-theme-shop

**Shop (example)**, a theme for [Polysite](https://github.com/polysites/polysite): a warm, paper-like look for a small
local shop's **catalogue** site. There is no cart and no checkout anywhere in it: products are a gallery and a price list,
and visitors are invited to visit or send an enquiry.

Kraft-paper background with a faint grain, serif headings, a striped awning, taped-up polaroid pictures, stitched edges and a
chalkboard for the opening hours. Light and dark variants, reduced-motion friendly, mobile first, AA text contrast.

## What it shows

- `gallery` as taped-up product photos with name and price captions; `price-list` with dotted leaders and stamped tags
  (new, local, seasonal) for the full catalogue; `hours-contact` as a chalkboard; a closing `cta` that points at the enquiry
  form; a gift card page and an enquiry form; an about page with a `timeline` and `team`.
- Seasonal highlights on the home page are just a gallery and a short price list, so staff change them in the admin.

## Demo site

`showcase/site.json` is a complete fictional shop ("Tuppence & Thistle": five pages, two forms, twelve illustrated products
and other generated SVG artwork in `showcase/images/`) to load as its own demo site. Everything in it is made up.

## Use it

Mount this folder as `themes/polysite-theme-shop/` in a Polysite checkout, restart the web and CMS containers, then choose
**Shop (example)** for a site under **Sites** in the admin. Turn the Donate button off for the site.
